# Acid print

Generated for Anvil with the built-in image generator. The final panel samples the masthead from this original. The step plots, list pages, status and controls are drawn by the instrument.

Use case: ui-mockup. Make a real acid-rave graphic identity for the MPC percussion instrument ANVIL, not a plain contemporary software UI. A creative designer's 320x218 flat front-on instrument screen in landscape, sharp retro low-resolution demoscene art. Dominant visual is top quarter: large LEGIBLE bespoke ANVIL chrome racing logotype with pinched serifs, blade terminals, lime facing and a thin ultraviolet offset extrusion on deep black, flanked by a folded checkerboard that spirals through a wireframe impact ring, one four-point chrome specular star. Design an ORIGINAL Y2K club flyer world in miniature. Pixel-screenprint high contrast, sharply cut ramps. Reserve right end of header for STEPS and RUN 05/08. Main picture is a working 8-step music display, structurally calm and functional with thin lavender rules, column numbers 01..08, distinct 'PITCH' height per step above zero and tapered striped 'VEL' velocity impulse per step below, pale brackets for picked step 03, a single fluorescent lime cap for playing step 05. Footer three compact dark cards K1 Pitch 3, K2 Velocity 3, K3 Cutoff with values and thin track colored rails. Very dark plum-black, acid yellow-green, ultraviolet shadows, ivory chrome on logo. Cropped-to-screen edges, no case, no margin. No borrowed text, smiley logos, manufacturer marks, sponsor names, watermark, taglines, slogans, grunge or irrelevant filler. The art and futuristic lettering should deliver a real rave flyer aesthetic at first glance, not just green text on a dark UI.
