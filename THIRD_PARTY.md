# Anvil screen credits

The ANVIL lettering, checker ribbon and wire ring are original artwork made for this instrument with the built-in image generator. The screen embeds a 238 × 55 pixel, sixteen-ink reduction of the masthead. The supplied visual references informed the direction. [Original screen study](assets/explore/acid-print.png) · [Prompt](assets/explore/direction.md) · [Native art](assets/art/anvil-masthead.png) · [Reduction script](tools/ui_preview/art.py)

Departure Mono by Helena Zhang supplies the 7 × 12 interface glyphs under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. [Font](assets/fonts/DepartureMono-Regular.otf) · [Licence](licenses/OFL-departure-mono.txt)

The preview compositor takes its soft keys from a capture of Melodee running the Anvil plug-in. The parameter displays and sequence graphics are rendered by Anvil itself.
